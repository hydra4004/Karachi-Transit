Transit, made clearer

Karachi Transit

Find a route, know when your bus is due, and plan a journey with confidence. A commuter app and union portal prototype, with simulated vehicles, ETAs, and service updates.

Commuter app (mobile)

Five tabs — Home, Map, Routes, Planner, More. Supports the four evaluation tasks end to end: find a route, track a bus and read its ETA, plan a two-vehicle trip, send and cancel an SOS, report a complaint.

Suggested demo script (5 minutes)

  1. Open the commuter app and the union portal side by side in two tabs.
  2. Routes → choose B13 to open the route, then open the map and tap a bus to see its next stop and ETA badge.
  3. Map → in the Selected routes row tick B13 and C1: only those two routes, the stops they serve and their vehicles stay on the map. Tap All to put every route back.
  4. Planner → tap the Sea View → Korangi chip: two vehicles, one transfer at PECHS, with the reason for the change printed on each step.
  5. Safety → press and hold the SOS button for 2 seconds, then cancel during the countdown.
  6. Union portal → Fleet → pick a bus → set it delayed or out of service, reassign the driver if needed, then publish; the commuter app shows the route alert immediately.
  7. More → switch the language to Urdu: the whole layout flips to right-to-left. Turn on Light mode or High contrast for daylight, and Simulate connection loss to see the last known position with its age.

Documentation and tests

The Phase 2 write-up sits in docs/ next to this folder: 01-design-requirements, 02-conceptual-design, 03-hci-principles, 04-traceability-matrix, 05-wireframes, 06-evaluation-plan, 07-iteration-log, 08-phase1-evidence-todo, 09-prototype-guide, 10-technical-reference and the assembled PHASE2-SUBMISSION. Serve the repository root to open them in the browser.

The prototype has 334 automated checks: prototype/tests/engine.test.js (221, Node), prototype/tests/ui.test.html (88, browser) and prototype/tests/sync.test.html (15, browser). Run them all with powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File prototype/tests/run-tests.ps1.

Running locally. Serve this folder over HTTP so that the union portal and the commuter app can talk to each other (they share state through localStorage): python -m http.server 8080 in this folder, then open http://localhost:8080. Opening the files directly with file:// works for the app itself, but the two apps may not stay in sync.
